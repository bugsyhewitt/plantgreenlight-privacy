Privacy Policy

Plant Green Light

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Plant Green Light collects no data. None.

Plant Green Light ("the App") is a simple utility app that displays a green light on your screen. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Information we collect

The App itself does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not use analytics or tracking, does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device data, and does not use cookies.

Optional tips via Google Play

The App includes an optional tip jar (menu → Donate). If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details, and the App keeps no record of it. A tip unlocks nothing, and the App never asks.

Third-party services

Other than Google Play's billing service for the optional tips described above, the App does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the App collects no data from any user, there is no data to be collected from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
